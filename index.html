Three quick steps

Opening from TikTok or Instagram? Their built-in browser can't sign you in to Google. Tap ⋯ (top right) and choose Open in browser, or copy this link into Chrome.

1Join the tester group

Use the same Google account that's signed in on your Android phone. Google shows a Join group button – tap it and you're in.

Join Google Group ↗

2Say yes to testing

Open the invite and tap Become a tester.

Open the invite ↗

If it says you're not eligible yet, wait a couple of minutes after step 1 and refresh.

3Install and play

Once step 2 is done, the Play Store page below will open for you. Try a round or two with your little one.

Get it on Google Play ↗
